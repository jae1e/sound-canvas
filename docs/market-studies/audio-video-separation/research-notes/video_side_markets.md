# Video-side AI media markets (research date: 2026-11-04)

Labels: [VF] verified fact (primary or multiple reliable sources), [VC] vendor claim, [EST] third-party market-research estimate, [HYP] hypothesis. Search-snippet-level evidence; most items were not re-fetched from primary pages, so treat [VF] as "reported by cited outlet".

## Person- and region-aware video enhancement

### Takeaway
Consumer/prosumer AI upscaling is a crowded, subscription-priced category (Topaz, NVIDIA RTX Video, TV SoCs) with weakly reliable market-size numbers (~$0.2-0.7B in 2025, [EST]). Broadcast archive remastering in Korea is project/service-based (SKT, 4BY4, Pixtree/SBS). I found no confirmed "face-aware" feature at Samsung/LG.

### Cited Findings
- [VF/secondary] Topaz Video AI is subscription-only in 2026; sources conflict on price: $299/yr standalone vs $67/month ($396/yr); Studio bundle $399/yr. Several are affiliate blogs; check Topaz page. — [Renderahouse](https://www.renderahouse.com/blog/topaz-ai-pricing), [MagicHour](https://magichour.ai/blog/topaz-video-ai-pricing), [Krea](https://www.krea.ai/blog/is-topaz-ai-free-pricing-system-requirements-and-alternatives-in-2026)

- [VC] Samsung 2026 Micro RGB TVs use "Micro RGB AI Engine Pro" with 4K AI upscaling; LG Micro RGB evo uses Alpha 11 AI Processor Gen 3 with "Dual Super Upscaling". — [Samsung](https://news.samsung.com/sg/samsung-electronics-introduces-2026-ai-tvs-redefining-home-entertainment-with-smarter-experiences), [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/samsung-and-lg-set-for-micro-rgb-led-tv-showdown-at-ces-2026/), [Cepro](https://www.cepro.com/news/samsung-expands-micro-rgb-tv-lineup-for-2026-with-new-sizes-and-enhanced-ai-performance/624261)

### Inferences
- [HYP] Archive remastering is a services/pilot business with few public prices; a small CV team would likely sell per-title or per-hour processing plus QC rather than SaaS seats.
- [HYP] The "person/face-aware" differentiator (restoration without identity drift) is a plausible niche versus generic upscalers, but unverified as a buyer-priced need.

### Gaps
- No primary-source confirmation of Adobe, CapCut, Runway enhancement pricing/features (not searched in depth).
- No evidence of face-aware picture modes at Samsung/LG; Maxine face features are documented mainly for video calls.
- No Korean broadcaster remaster contract values or per-minute prices found.
- Market-size reports disagree by ~4x and are low-reliability.

## Virtual ad and billboard replacement in sports broadcasting

### Takeaway
Consolidating: TGI Sport acquired Supponor (July 2024, terms undisclosed) after buying Brand Brigade; uniqFEED (Zurich) is the main independent, with Bundesliga league-wide approval (June 2025). Mirriad (in-content placement) is in severe financial distress.

### Gaps

## Video compression and encoding vendors at trade shows

### Takeaway
Show traffic is flat-to-down (IBC 2025: 43,858; IBC 2026: 41,225; NAB 2026: ~58,000), but compression stays relevant because bitrate cuts convert directly into CDN/bandwidth savings. The sector is consolidating: Harmonic sold its video business, InterDigital bought Deep Render, Sony bought iSIZE.

### Cited Findings
- [VF] IBC2025 (12-15 Sep, Amsterdam): 43,858 visitors, 1,300+ exhibitors; 2024 was 45,085. — [TV Technology](https://www.tvtechnology.com/news/ibc2025-attracts-43-858-visitors), [SVG Europe](https://www.svgeurope.org/blog/headlines/ibc-reveals-2025-attendee-and-exhibitor-numbers/)
- [VF] IBC2026: 41,225 attendees from 170 countries, 1,300+ exhibitors, 256 debut companies, ~6% down; next show 10-13 Sep 2027. Coverage themes: AI, live sport economics; compression not mentioned. — [TV Technology](https://www.tvtechnology.com/events/ibc2026-attracted-41-225-attendees-from-170-countries)

Outlets differ on first-timer share (48% vs 44%). — [SVG](https://www.sportsvideo.org/2026/04/23/nab-reports-58000-registered-attendees-at-2026-nab-show-up-from-2025-but-down-from-2024/), [TV Technology](https://www.tvtechnology.com/events/the-2026-nab-show-wraps-with-more-than-58-000-attendees)

## Where audio meets video

### Takeaway
Gaudio Lab's Gaudio Studio Pro combines stem separation, dubbing, subtitling, music replacement, lip-sync, mixing/mastering; Flawless does visual dubbing. I found no confirmed named video partner for Gaudio Lab.

### Cited Findings
- [VC] Gaudio Studio Pro (GSEP-based) won two CES 2026 Innovation Awards; workflow covers separation, dubbing, subtitles, music replacement, lip-sync; Gaudio exhibited at NAB 2026 and WIS 2026 Seoul. — [audioXpress](https://audioxpress.com/news/gaudio-lab-wins-two-ces-2026-innovation-awards-for-gaudio-studio-pro-ai-audio-solution), [Wowtale](https://en.wowtale.net/2025/11/05/232620)
- [VF/secondary] Flawless (TrueSync, DeepEditor): total funding estimates $33.5M-$39.9M, latest dated round May 2024 (Caplight); no 2025 round found. — [Caplight](https://www.caplight.com/company/flawless), [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/ai-startup-flawless-edits-hollywood-movies-dubbing-actors)

### Inferences
- [HYP] The audio+video gap: lip-synced dubbing plus separated stems plus on-screen text/object edits in one pipeline; Gaudio covers audio, Flawless visual; nobody confirmed doing both.

### Gaps
- Gaudio Lab video partners, Flawless 2025-26 financials/revenue, other dual-modality firms not verified.

## "블러미" as a Korean service

### Takeaway
Identified with good confidence: 블러미 (BlurMe / BlurMe Enterprise) by Zarasoft (자라소프트), an AI face and license-plate de-identification product for video, images, PDFs.

### Cited Findings
- [VC/press release, 2026-07-15] Zarasoft's BlurMe Enterprise auto-detects and blurs faces and license plates in video/photos and removes personal data in documents; browser-based, server GPU processing, centrally managed; on-prem (closed network) and cloud versions; free trial at blur.me. — [Kair News](https://www.kairnews.com/news/504664), [Newswire](https://www.newswire.co.kr/newsRead.php?no=1038584)
- [VF] Designated 조달청 우수조달물품 (AI software category, no. 2026068) on 2026-07-13; buyable via 나라장터 digital service mall by sole-source contract (item ID 25981123). Price not stated. — [Kair News](https://www.kairnews.com/news/504664)
- [VC] Customers: Seoul Jongno-gu Office (adopted Dec 2025, CCTV disclosure requests), KORAIL stations; boilerplate also claims 500+ US school districts. Service flow: public body gets CCTV disclosure request; previously staff manually mosaic frame-by-frame; BlurMe auto-detects. — same sources

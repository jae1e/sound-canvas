## Gaudio Lab as a company (products, awards, customers, funding, model)

### Takeaway
Gaudio Lab is a Seoul-based B2B audio-AI company (founded 2015) that moved from licensing loudness/spatial-audio SDKs to consumer devices and music services, to selling an OTT localisation suite (Gaudio Studio Pro, with DME Separator and Music Replacer) plus a pay-per-minute API. Product and award facts are well documented by the company; funding is stale (last public round Oct 2021, Series B 11.3bn KRW) and no revenue, headcount or IPO information was found.

## Why DME separation from a finished master is needed

### Takeaway
The need chain is verified at the delivery-spec level: Netflix requires a fully filled M&E (and DME splits or stems in other contexts), and distributors need M&E for dubbing, so a missing M&E blocks export. Direct, dated, quantified evidence for the Korean-specific claims (archive-only masters, KOMCA blanket licences not covering worldwide use, BGM replaced on OTT) was largely NOT found; they remain plausible but only partly sourced.

### Cited Findings
- Netflix M&E guidelines: deliver a fully filled Music & Effects package as long-play discrete channels, conformed to final media; do not modify music levels; Foley is critical to a fully filled M&E [FACT, official spec] — [Netflix M&E Creation Guidelines](https://backlothelp.netflix.com/hc/en-us/articles/115006122187-M-E-Creation-Guidelines)
- Netflix nonfiction guidance: build the M&E from dipped stems and elements; M&E is an audio mix excluding program dialogue for localisation or marketing [FACT] — [Nonfiction M&E guidelines](https://backlothelp.netflix.com/hc/en-us/articles/360057558533-M-E-Creation-and-Delivery-Guidelines-for-Nonfiction), [Backlot delivery instructions](https://backlothelp.netflix.com/hc/en-us/articles/223351987)
- Titles mixed natively in 2.0 need a separate fully filled 2.0 stereo M&E submix; dubbing archival specs list a fully filled M&E matching the primary mix channel configuration [FACT] — [Near Field 2.0 spec](https://backlothelp.netflix.com/hc/en-us/articles/23506749090323-Near-Field-2-0-Stereo-Delivery-Specifications-and-Guidelines-for-Titles-Mixed-Natively-in-2-0), [Dubbing archival specs](https://backlothelp.netflix.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002110287-Dubbing-and-Audio-Description-Archival-Asset-Specifications)
- Netflix acknowledges that a fully filled M&E may be unavailable and DME splits or OMF/AAF may be provided instead (trailer-creation context) [FACT] — [Netflix search result summary, snippet only; see Netflix help centre](https://backlothelp.netflix.com/hc/en-us/articles/223351987)
- A distributor-side post-production vendor: distributors need an M&E so foreign studios can dub; without one the film cannot be sold to foreign buyers; muting dialogue leaves "digital silence", Foley/ambience must be rebuilt; sung vocals count as dialogue, and removing vocals afterwards "usually results in a muddy, unusable mess"; dialogue spill can fail QC [VENDOR/trade explainer, undated] — [Travsonic](https://www.travsonic.com/mne-for-film-distribution/)
- Another vendor says M&E tracks are often missing or never created, especially for archival content, and AI separation can generate them (AudioShake glossary, a competitor) [VENDOR, snippet only] — [AudioShake](https://www.audioshake.ai/glossary/m-and-e-music-and-effects)
- Gaudio's stated reasons OTT needs DME separation: localisation (D/ME), music-licence expiry (DE/M), upmixing legacy content to 5.1/Atmos, clean dialogue vs singing voices [VENDOR, 9 Jan 2026] — [Gaudio blog 231](https://www.gaudiolab.com/blog/231)
- Korean licensing context: KOMCA and Netflix signed a licence renewal covering 2021-2025 usage; earlier 2018 agreement; Netflix reportedly paid 2.5% of Korean revenue per KOMCA, while domestic OTTs argued for the broadcast re-transmission rate (0.6% or less) and the culture ministry set about 1.5% (2020-2021 reporting) [FACT as press reports; dispute is about domestic OTT royalty rates, NOT about worldwide export clearance] — [MTN 2020](https://news.mtn.co.kr/news-detail/2020121111095297866), [eToday](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/1973002), [Asia Economy 2025-03](https://view.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2025030410374222191)
- K-drama music clearance: a Reddit r/KDRAMA wiki says only songs written and recorded for the drama are easy to license worldwide; pre-existing songs are hard, and background music is swapped for generic tracks on Netflix; the wiki and fan posts list specific edits (a bar-scene BGM removed, a ringtone song replaced) [ANECDOTE, undated community wiki] — [r/KDRAMA Netflix FAQ mirror](https://reddit.birdcat.cafe/r/KDRAMA/wiki/kdramas_101/netflix_faqs)
- Foley/M&E cost: the Travsonic article says full-length Foley "can easily run into the thousands of dollars" with no exact figure; one distributor-side source warns that distributors offering to fix a missing M&E charge very high rates [VENDOR, snippet only] — [Travsonic](https://www.travsonic.com/mne-for-film-distribution/), [Enhanced Media](https://enhanced.media/blog/2021/3/2/how-to-stop-wasting-money-on-your-films-distribution-costs)
- Manual music replacement time: Gaudio's partner programme text says ~1 month manual versus >90% reduction; testimonial 1 month+ versus ~3 days for 60 min [VENDOR] — see Unicorn Factory and GSP overview above
- Virtual advertising analogy: not researched in detail; [HYP] it is structurally similar in that rights-restricted regions of finished video are replaced per territory (virtual ad insertion in sports broadcasts), but no source was collected.

### Inferences
- The logic "stems should already exist" is only partly true: Netflix expects delivered M&E for new productions, but for legacy catalogue titles, third-party-owned or on-air-only masters, the multitrack or M&E is often unavailable (vendor claims), which is the market Gaudio targets [HYP, supported by vendor claims only].
- Music clearance is the likelier driver for Korean content: if domestic BGM was cleared only for domestic broadcast, an OTT/overseas deal needs different rights or replacement; separation of music from dialogue/effects then makes replacement feasible without a full remix [HYP; the first half is not verified with a KOMCA scope document].
- Cost of manual alternatives is poorly documented publicly; $/hour numbers must come from quotes.

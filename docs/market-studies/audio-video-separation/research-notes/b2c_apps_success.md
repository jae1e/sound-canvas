## Moises (Music.AI): downloads, users, awards, funding, revenue, pricing

### Takeaway
Moises is the clear consumer leader: 70M registered users by end of 2025 [VENDOR], $40M Series A in Jan 2025 [FACT], Apple iPad App of the Year 2024. The widely repeated "40M+ downloads" figure was NOT verified. The company reports registered users, not downloads, and publishes no revenue, ARR, or subscriber count.

### Cited Findings
- Registered-user trajectory (all [VENDOR], company-reported): 4M (Aug 2021, with $1.6M seed), 20M+ (Aug 2022, $8.65M round), 30M (Apr 2023), 50M (Jan 2025), 70M (Dec 2025, "over 15 million new creators" in 2025) — [Bloomberg Linea 2021](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/english/moises-the-chart-topping-music-app-by-a-brazilian-founder-raises-16-million-in-an-oversubscribed-round/); [Moises milestone, $8.65M](https://moises.ai/newsroom/company-milestones/moises-raises-8-65-million); [MBW, 30M](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/after-rasing-10-25m-ai-music-making-app-moises-reaches-than-more-30m-registered-users1/); [MBW, 50M](https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/music-ai-raises-40m-in-series-a-round-as-its-moises-platform-hits-50m-users); [Moises 2025 year in review](https://moises.ai/newsroom/company-milestones/2025-year-in-review)
- 2025 year-in-review also claims 1.2B minutes of audio processed, 33 languages, 190 countries; top markets US, Brazil, India, Mexico, UK [VENDOR]; "Raised $50+ million" [VENDOR]; downloads and subscribers "not reported" on the page — [Moises 2025 year in review](https://moises.ai/newsroom/company-milestones/2025-year-in-review)

### Inferences
- [HYP] Moises likely converts a low-single-digit percent of registered users; 70M registered users x 2-4% x ~$50-100/yr would imply tens to low hundreds of $M, which is far above the $3.5-16M third-party estimates, suggesting either lower conversion/churn-heavy cohorts or that registered users are a very loose metric. Treat as unresolved.
- Registered users (cumulative signups) are not MAU; the best category benchmark is therefore an upper bound.

## LALAL.AI, vocalremover.org, UVR, Splitter.ai, Fadr, RipX

### Takeaway
Free web vocal removers have large traffic (vocalremover.org roughly 9.5M monthly visits per a directory [3P-EST], undated), while LALAL.AI is smaller (~2.4-2.6M) but monetizes with minute-based subscriptions. Exact Similarweb numbers with month were not obtainable.

### Cited Findings
- LALAL.AI pricing [FACT, vendor price page, fetched 2026-11-04]: Starter free (10 min relaxed queue, 200MB files); Lite $7.5/mo or $90/yr (unlimited relaxed, 90 fast min/mo, 2GB); Pro $15/mo or $180/yr (250 fast min/mo, VST plugin). Minute top-ups: 750 min $50, 3,000 min $190, 5,000 min $300. Minutes = file length x number of stem types; fast minutes do not roll over; non-refundable — [LALAL.AI pricing](https://www.lalal.ai/pricing/)
- Traffic [3P-EST]: vocalremover.org ~9.5M monthly visits and LALAL.AI ~2.4M (directory, no month) — [seektool.ai](https://seektool.ai/en/ai/vocalremover-org/alternatives); Semrush snapshot Dec 2025: lalal.ai 2.59M visits, 65.17% bounce — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/vocalremover.org/competitors/). Similarweb lists lalal.ai, vocalremover.org, x-minus.pro, moises.ai as mutual competitors (Mar-Apr 2026) but the visit totals were not shown — [Similarweb vocalremover.org](https://www.similarweb.com/website/vocalremover.org/competitors); [Similarweb lalal.ai](https://www.similarweb.com/website/lalal.ai/competitors/)
- RipX DAW (Hit'n'Mix): one-time $99 (RipX DAW) / $198 (Pro), 21-day trial [FACT, vendor] — [Hit'n'Mix](https://www.hitnmix.com/buy-ripx/). A blog's $14.99/mo subscription claim conflicts and is unreliable.
- Gaudio Studio exists as a phone app that separates vocals, bass, drums, guitar, piano — [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Gaudio-Studio-arrives-on-smartphones-Extract-individual-vocal-and-instrument-stems-from-songs-for-remixing-and-sing-alongs.1045627.0.html)

### Inferences
- [HYP] Free ad-supported sites capture volume; paid minute-metered plans ($7.5-15/mo) are the main B2C monetization; the per-minute framing exposes GPU cost directly.

### Gaps
- Similarweb exact monthly visits (with month) for lalal.ai, vocalremover.org, moises.ai, splitter.ai: not retrieved (page content truncated).
- UVR (open-source, free), Fadr, Splitter.ai pricing and business model: searches returned nothing usable.

## Platform features (Apple, Spotify, Samsung, TikTok/CapCut, YouTube, karaoke apps)

### Takeaway
Platforms are adding narrow, licensed or reactive features: Apple Music Sing (Dec 2022), YouTube Erase Song (2024, reactive to claims), Spotify/UMG licensed AI remix add-on (announced May 2026, no date/price). None offers general consumer stem export, so standalone apps remain differentiated.

### Cited Findings
- Apple Music Sing: shipped with iOS/iPadOS/tvOS 16.2 (Dec 2022); requires non-Voice-Plan Apple Music subscription; adjustable lead-vocal volume with on-screen lyrics and duet view; Apple told TechCrunch iPhone 11 and later, iPads with A13 or later, Apple TV 4K (2022); described as AI-based real-time vocal/instrument separation (press description) — [9to5Mac compatibility](https://9to5mac.com/2022/12/08/apple-music-sing-karaoke-compatible-devices); [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/22/12/07/hands-on-with-apple-music-sing-on-iphone-ipad-apple-tv). Device lists differ across sources (SE).
- Spotify vocal removal: no confirmed global feature as of mid-2026. A vendor blog (StemSplit, commercially interested) says a vocal-reduction "Sing" mode exists in Japan via Joysound — UNVERIFIED by Spotify/Joysound — [StemSplit](https://stemsplit.io/blog/spotify-karaoke-mode). Spotify lyrics overhaul 2026 is karaoke-style highlighting only.
- Spotify x UMG: licensing deal (reported May 2026) for fan AI covers/remixes as paid Premium add-on; no price or launch date at the time; earlier (2025) Bloomberg-reported "Music Pro" tier at up to $5.99 more/month with AI remix tools (report, unconfirmed) — [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/spotify-and-universal-music-group-strike-landmark-deal-to-let-fans-create-ai-covers-and-remixes-as-a-paid-premium-add-on/); [The Music Network](https://themusicnetwork.com/news/spotify-universal-music-ai-covers-deal); [MBW report](https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/spotify-to-launch-music-pro-service-with-superfan-perks-like-early-access-tickets-and-ai-remix-tool-for-up-to-5-99-more-per-month-report). Launch status after May 2026 not checked.
- YouTube "Erase song": updated July 2024; in YouTube Studio Video Copyright page, "Select Action", removes claimed music while keeping other audio; can fail; usable only after a claim — [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2024/07/05/youtube-erase-song-tool); [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/103713-youtube-unveils-ai-powered-tool-cleanly-remove-copyrighted.html). Relevance: BGM removal for creators is already free and built in.
- Samsung: Audio Eraser (Galaxy S25, 2025) separates voices/music/noise in recorded video; no song-stem or vocal-removal feature found — [SamMobile](https://www.sammobile.com/news/galaxy-s25-audio-eraser-what-how-it-works/)
- CapCut: pages market a free AI vocal remover / voice isolator with up to 10 stems [VENDOR]; "Trusted by TikTok" is a badge, not proof of TikTok integration — [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/resource/voice-isolator); [CapCut vocal remover](https://www.capcut.com/create/ai-vocal-remover)

## Revenue model and unit economics

### Takeaway
Observed models: freemium subscription (LALAL.AI $7.5-15/mo with minute quotas), one-time license (RipX $99-198), free ad-supported web. Conversion rates and GPU cost per minute were not found publicly.

### Cited Findings
- LALAL.AI meters by minutes x stems and sells top-ups at about $0.067/min (3,000 min for $190 = $0.063; 750 for $50 = $0.067; 5,000 for $300 = $0.06), computed from [LALAL.AI pricing](https://www.lalal.ai/pricing/)
- Moises "profitable" claim (unverified, above).

### Inferences
- [HYP] A ~$0.06/min retail price for fast-queue implies cloud GPU cost per audio-minute is well below that; on-device inference would turn marginal cost to near zero, making free-tier generosity a strategic lever.

### Gaps
- Public conversion rates, cloud GPU cost per minute, on-device benchmarks: not found.

## Copyright and legal issues

### Takeaway
No lawsuit specifically against a consumer stem splitter was found. Legal action targets generative AI (Suno/Udio, June 2024); AudioShake positions as licensed, with major label/publisher customers.

### Cited Findings
- RIAA-backed labels sued Suno and Udio June 2024 — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2024/06/24/record-labels-sue-ai-startups-copyright-infringement)
- AudioShake: models trained on licensed production-library music (CEO interview); users listed include Universal, Warner, Sony, Disney, Concord, Downtown, peermusic (Sep 2025 company post) [VENDOR] — [Kill The DJ](https://killthedj.com/audioshake-interview); [AudioShake](https://www.audioshake.ai/post/audioshake-debuts-new-instrument-separation-models)
- Spotify-UMG licensed remix deal shows label stance: permit transformation only under license and revenue share (May 2026) — [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/spotify-and-universal-music-group-strike-landmark-deal-to-let-fans-create-ai-covers-and-remixes-as-a-paid-premium-add-on/)
- A CMU article, "Stem wars: recent lawsuits expose copyright pitfalls for labels and producers" (2024), exists but body not read — [CMU](https://completemusicupdate.com/tag/jon-hume)

### Inferences
- [HYP] User-uploaded personal-use stem separation has so far drawn little enforcement; risk rises with any catalog-integrated or streaming-linked product.

### Gaps
- Any label/publisher takedown or suit against LALAL.AI, vocalremover.org, UVR, Moises: none found (absence in limited search, not proof).

## Defining "success" in this category (numbers observed)

- Top tier: Moises, 70M registered users (Dec 2025), $50M+ raised, awards; revenue undisclosed (estimates $3.5-16M, low confidence).
- Mid tier: LALAL.AI about 2.4-2.6M monthly web visits with paid plans $7.5-15/mo.
- Mass free tier: vocalremover.org about 9.5M monthly visits [3P-EST].
- Platform bundles: Apple Music Sing, YouTube Erase Song, free tools in CapCut.
- [HYP] Practical benchmark: millions of registered users plus a profitable sub-$20M ARR business, not a venture-scale outcome, unless attached to a creator tool or licensed platform.

Voice Coach UI v2

The two coaches have different jobs, so they get different layouts. Sing is a dark stage: the live pitch and waveform fill the screen, like a tuner and a voice recorder in one. Speak is a light desk: the time, the plan and the current section lead, and the voice graph is a small strip. Both use the same parts: the audio bar, the sheets, the record button and the take review.

  • Three tabs: 노래 (Sing), 말하기 (Speak), 기록 (Takes). Settings is the gear icon at the top right.
  • One primary action on each screen. Everything else is a chip that opens a sheet.
  • Input and output are always visible at the top of both recorders.

The written design and its reasons are in voice-coach-ux-v2.md. These pages show the screens. Colours, sizes and type are in the design tokens.

Sing: a dark stage for the voice

The pitch graph is the main element. The note name is large enough to read at arm's length. The waveform turns coral while recording, so the user sees at a glance that the take is live. The beat dots appear only when the beat is on.

Speak: a light desk for the talk

A speaker cares about time, the plan, and pace. So the timer and the cue card lead, and the voice graph is a thin strip at the bottom. The layout changes fully between "before", "during" and "after", because each stage asks a different question.

Design tokens and rules for the implementation

TokenSing (dark)Speak, Takes, Settings (light)
Background#0E1117#F6F5F1
Surface / border#171C25 / #262D3A#FFFFFF / #ECE9E2
Text / muted#E8EBF1 / #6B7486#1B1D22 / #6C6F78
Accentteal #5EEAD4 (pitch line, active tab)indigo #4F46E5
Record#FF3B30 everywhere. Waveform while recording: #FF6B6B
Good / caution / bad#34D399 or #16A34A · #FBBF24 or #B45309 · #F87171 or #E5484D (light uses the darker value)
TypeSystem font (Roboto and Noto Sans KR). Note 58sp/800 · speech clock 66sp/800 · screen title 21sp/800 · card title 21sp/800 · body 14sp · caption 12sp. Numbers use tabular figures.
Spacing and shapeSteps of 4dp. Screen padding 16dp. Card radius 20dp, pill radius 20dp, sheet top radius 24dp. Touch targets of 48dp or more. The record button is 80dp with a 4dp ring.
Motion and feedbackEvery pressable: ripple plus scale 0.97. Record to stop: the circle morphs to a rounded square in 180 ms. Result card: slides up in 220 ms. Graphs: 30 fps or more, drawn natively or with Skia, never with React state for each frame.
  • Tab bar colours follow the screen: dark on Sing, white on the others. The tab bar hides while a speech take records.
  • The screen stays on while recording. Leaving the screen or the app stops and saves the take.
  • Empty states use words: "소리를 내 보세요" (make a sound) and "아직 녹음이 없어요" (no recordings yet). Never a blank area.
